## Decision

## Rationale

## Consequences

### Positive

- Positive consequence.

### Negative and trade-offs

### Neutral or follow-up

## Security, Privacy, and Data Impact

Describe changed trust boundaries, sensitive data, authorization, secrets, retention, failure modes, and compliance implications. Write “None” only after review.

## Operational Impact

Describe deployment, observability, backup, recovery, cost, support, and team-knowledge impact.

## Validation

State how the decision will be tested or evaluated and what evidence is required before rollout.

## Review Triggers

List events or measurements that require reconsideration.
